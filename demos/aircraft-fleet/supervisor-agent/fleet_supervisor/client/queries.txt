# Neo4j Aviation Database - Multi-Agent Routing Test Queries
# These queries test routing to Maintenance and Operations specialist agents.
#
# Query Categories:
# - Maintenance Domain (1-18): Faults, components, sensors, reliability, manuals
# - Operations Domain (19-33): Flights, delays, routes, airports, fleet discovery
#
# Each query should route to the appropriate specialist agent.

# === Maintenance Domain (Routes to Maintenance Agent) ===

1. What are the most common maintenance faults across all aircraft?

2. Show me all critical severity maintenance events and which aircraft they affected.

3. Which components have the most failures? Are there any patterns by component type?

4. Find aircraft with hydraulic system issues and show the faults and corrective actions.

5. What maintenance events have affected the CFM56-7B engines? Show severity and fault details.

6. Analyze sensor readings for engine temperature sensors - what is the range of values?

7. Which sensors are showing abnormal readings that might indicate maintenance needs?

8. What is the reliability history of avionics components in the fleet?

9. Show me the maintenance events with the highest severity.

10. Which aircraft have had repeated failures of the same component type?

11. Show me all the systems installed on Boeing 737-800 aircraft.

12. What sensors are monitoring engine systems across the fleet and what do they measure?

13. For ExampleAir's Boeing 737-800 fleet, trace the path from aircraft to systems to components to sensors and show the full hierarchy.

# This one spans both domains. The supervisor should call Maintenance and Operations.
14. Which aircraft have both maintenance events AND flight delays? Show the correlation between maintenance issues and operational delays.

# Manual questions. Only the Maintenance Agent can search the manuals.
15. What is the procedure for detecting a hydraulic system leak?

16. How do I troubleshoot an engine exhaust gas temperature exceedance?

17. What does the maintenance manual recommend for hydraulic reservoir low-level warnings?

18. For maintenance events whose fault is 'Vibration exceedance', what corrective actions were taken, and what does the manual say the correct engine-vibration troubleshooting procedure is?

# === Operations Domain (Routes to Operations Agent) ===

19. What are the most common causes of flight delays and how many minutes do they add?

20. Which routes have the most delays? Show origin, destination, and total delay minutes.

21. Compare delay statistics by operator - which airline has the worst on-time performance?

22. Find all weather-related delays longer than 60 minutes and show affected flights.

23. What flights depart from JFK airport and where do they go?

24. Which airports have the highest traffic volume in our database?

25. Show me the busiest routes by number of flights operated.

26. What is the on-time performance for flights to LAX?

27. Which carriers operate the most flights in the system?

28. Find flights with the longest scheduled duration and their routes.

29. What is the database schema? Show me all node types and their relationships.

30. How many aircraft are in the fleet and what models do we have?

31. List all airports in the database with their IATA codes and cities.

32. Which manufacturers build the aircraft in our fleet and how many of each?

33. What aircraft does SkyWays operate and what are their tail numbers?
